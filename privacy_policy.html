RiseUp · Privacy Policy

What RiseUp does with your data

The short version: RiseUp cannot see your data, because it has no way to send it anywhere. This page explains exactly what's stored, where, and why — permission by permission.

Last updated · August 22, 2026 App · RiseUp (com.buddy.riseup) Contact · gnanesh@neosan.in

RiseUp has no internet permission at all. It is not technically capable of sending anything — your alarms, your camera, your habits — to a server, because there is no server, and no network code exists to reach one.

01 Data we collect

None. RiseUp has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting service. Nothing about you or your device is transmitted anywhere, because the app has no network access to transmit it with — this isn't a policy choice we could quietly reverse later without you noticing, it's a missing permission in the app itself.

02 What's stored on your device

Everything RiseUp needs lives only in a local, encrypted database on your phone (AES-256, via SQLCipher), with the encryption key itself held in your device's hardware-backed Android Keystore — not in app code, not in a plain file.

DataWhereLeaves device?
Alarm times, titles, categories, mission settingsEncrypted local databaseNever
Mission completion history, streaks, XPEncrypted local databaseNever
Camera preview frames (Camera mission only)Analyzed in memory, immediately discardedNever

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this immediately and completely — there's no cloud copy to also go delete, because none exists.

03 The Camera mission, specifically

One alarm type asks you to move in front of the camera to dismiss it. RiseUp only measures brightness changes between frames to detect motion — it does not run face recognition, does not identify what's in frame, and does not save any photo, video, or frame to disk at any point. Each frame is discarded the instant the next one arrives. Camera access is requested only when you choose this mission type, and only used while that specific alarm is actively ringing.

Run at device startupSo alarms you've already set still fire after your phone restarts, instead of silently disappearing.

05 Third parties

There are none. No ad network, no analytics provider, no data broker, nobody. Nothing in this app is built to make money from your data, which is also why there's no reason for it to leave your phone in the first place.

06 Children's privacy

RiseUp doesn't collect data from anyone, including children, and isn't specifically directed at children under 13.

07 If this changes

It will, eventually — and this page will be updated first. If RiseUp adds Google Play subscriptions, purchase and billing information will be handled directly by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy — RiseUp itself still won't run its own servers or see your payment details. If cloud sync between devices is ever added, that's the point this policy would need real, substantive changes (what's synced, where it's stored, how it's encrypted in transit) — not a quiet edit. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the current, accurate state of the app.
